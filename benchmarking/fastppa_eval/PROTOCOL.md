# Evaluation protocol for fastppa

The purpose of this protocol is that the accuracy reported for a cost model
is measured on designs that played no part in choosing or fitting that
model.

## Data sets

- Calibration set C: single-operator designs generated by
  `gen_microbench.py`, at several widths and array sizes. Cost functions may
  be fitted, and their form chosen, on C only.
- Held-out set E1: all benchmarks of the LogikBench groups `basic`,
  `arithmetic`, `memory` and `blocks`, at LogikBench commit
  `58539b81d9c622a6b55a4cabde5d6b1288b9fe1e`, with their default parameters.
- Held-out set E3: all benchmarks of the LogikBench groups `epfl`,
  `iscas85`, `iscas89`, `koios` and `large`, at the same commit and with
  their default parameters. E3 was fixed on 2026-11-20, after decisions had
  been made by looking at E1 results (keeping `--netlist`, and the rules
  for conditional array writes and shifts by multiples of a constant), and
  before any model was run on it.
- Secondary set E2: the designs in `regression/fastppa/{blocks,designs}`.
  The model of PR #1927 was tuned on these designs, so for that model they
  are in sample, and are reported as such.

A design is excluded only for mechanical reasons: a model cannot parse or
elaborate it, or the ground-truth flow fails or takes longer than 600 s.
Every exclusion is listed with its reason. When models are compared, a
design that one of them cannot handle is excluded for all of them, and the
coverage of each model is reported.

## Ground truth

- G1 (primary): Yosys and ABC from the `openroad/orfs` image (`synth
  -flatten`, `dfflibmap` and `abc` with the NanGate45 typical liberty); area
  from `stat -liberty`. Delay from OpenSTA (in `openroad`) on the mapped
  netlist with the same liberty: a clock with a 50 ns period on the clock
  ports (a virtual clock for designs without registers), zero input and
  output delays, and the worst data arrival time over all path groups.
- G2 (secondary): LogikBench's published `yosys_freepdk45` results
  (`results/syn/asic/yosys_freepdk45.json`): cell area, and 1/fmax as the
  delay. These come from a flow outside our control.

## Quantities compared

Area is fastppa's total area. G1's delay is a data arrival time: it
includes the clock-to-Q delay of a launching register but not the setup
time of a capturing one. For the model of PR #1927, its "Reg-to-reg
delay" is compared, as in that PR's own comparison.

## Metrics

Per design, the ratio r = estimate / ground truth, for area and delay
separately, and the error factor max(r, 1/r). Over a set, these are
reported:
- the geometric mean of r, which measures bias;
- the median and 90th percentile of the error factor;
- the fraction of designs with an error factor of at most 1.5 and 2;
- Spearman's rank correlation between estimate and ground truth.

Designs with a zero estimate or ground truth (e.g., designs that synthesis
reduces to wires) are counted in the `zero` column and not in the ratios.

## Rules

- A model and its cost tables are frozen, and their commit recorded, before
  they are first run on E1. If a change is made after looking at E1
  results, E1 becomes in sample for the changed model. Its results are then
  reported as in sample, and out-of-sample claims need a new held-out set,
  fixed before it is first run.
- Results are produced by the scripts in this directory, and are stored
  together with the commit of the model and the versions of the
  ground-truth tools.
